OEM / ODM Workflow

From sample discussion to repeat bulk orders.

For custom, wholesale, or sample orders, useful details are specific: leather grade, glove pattern, reinforcement points, cuff style, lining, color, size run, logo placement, packaging and carton requirements.

  • Send reference photo, target market, estimated quantity and price level.
  • Confirm leather type, thickness, lining, cuff and reinforcement structure.
  • Review sample, adjust sizing and packaging, then move to production plan.
  • Keep reorder records by SKU, material, color and buyer packaging notes.
